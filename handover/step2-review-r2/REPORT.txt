FINDING 1: Typed blank pages now accumulate unbounded empty parts
SEVERITY: BLOCKING
WHERE: src-tauri/src/extract/readers/office/mod.rs:629,657; src-tauri/src/extract/mod.rs:176

FINDING 3: One XML text event still holds multiple huge copies
SEVERITY: BLOCKING
WHERE: src-tauri/src/extract/readers/office/mod.rs:409,674-676,785-789
EVIDENCE: r2_single_xml_text_memory: a 1,236,297-byte docx containing one 80 MiB text node with CR line endings:

FINDING 4: Repeated custom-style parsing remains an unchecked multiplicative loop

WHERE: src-tauri/src/extract/readers/office/excel.rs:67,228-235,281-319
